Suggested additions to the collection

Branded sew-in label

Woven/satin "uru" label stitched into every keepsake (now part of the finished-bag spec).

Maker's provenance card

Names the cluster & the woman who wove it — powers "Signed by Hand." Near-zero cost via §3 printers.

Seed-paper plantable tag

The hang tag becomes a gift — reinforces "no plastic / outlasts the day."

Muslin dust wrap

Cotton wrap around the keepsake inside the box — premium, plastic-free unboxing.

Engraved brass initials charm

Tied to the cord — cross-sell with BMP Labels; high perceived value.

Natural void fill

Dyed korai shred or dried marigold instead of wood-wool — on-brand, solves the no-TN gap.

Branded twill ribbon / wax seal

"uru" printed ribbon or wax seal for the box close.

Fragrance sachet

Incense/potpourri tucked in the weave (Sivakasi) — scent memory of the day.

Own hand-stamp

A rubber/brass stamp you own to press names/dates on kraft tags in-studio.
