## Sourcing model — the bag is ONE finished component

We buy the **woven keepsake as a complete, finished piece** — the weaver integrates the lining, accent band, tassel/drawcord, closure/zip and a sewn-in "uru" label. We do **not** source zippers, tassels, bands and linings as separate lines. Fabric/trim/closure vendors are kept only as a fallback "integration sub-suppliers" list (§1a) in case a chosen weaver can't finish in-house.

So the set breaks into just **six** supply lines:

## Suggested additions to the collection

1. **Branded woven/satin sew-in label** — a small "uru" label the weaver stitches into every keepsake (part of the finished-bag spec now).
2. **Maker's provenance card** — names the cluster & the woman who wove it (reinforces "Signed by Hand"). Same printers as §3.
3. **Seed-paper plantable tag** as the default hang tag — the tag itself becomes a gift (Pepaa / Reign Green).
4. **Muslin / cotton dust wrap** for the keepsake inside the box — premium, plastic-free unboxing.
5. **Engraved brass initials charm** tied to the cord — cross-sell with §2/§5 BMP Labels.
6. **Natural void fill = dyed korai shred or dried marigold** instead of wood-wool — on-brand, local, solves the no-TN-wood-wool gap.
7. **Branded twill ribbon or wax seal** ("uru") for the box close.
8. **Fragrance sachet** (incense/potpourri from Sivakasi) tucked in the weave — scent memory of the day.
9. **Own rubber/brass hand-stamp** to press names/dates on kraft tags in-studio — personalisation without a per-order vendor job.
