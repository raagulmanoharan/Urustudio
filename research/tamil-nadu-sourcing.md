## Suggested additions to the collection

Components worth adding to strengthen the set and the brand story:

1. **Branded woven/satin sew-in label** — a small "uru" label stitched into every keepsake (woven-label makers cluster in Tiruppur; ties into the Sowcarpet/Tiruppur trim lines above).
2. **Maker's provenance card** — a card naming the weaving cluster & the woman who made it (reinforces "Signed by Hand"). Same printers as §5; near-zero extra cost.
3. **Seed-paper plantable tag** as the default hang tag — the tag itself becomes a gift (Pepaa / Reign Green), reinforcing "no plastic" and "outlasts the day."
4. **Muslin / cotton dust wrap** for the keepsake inside the box — premium unboxing, plastic-free (Erode/Tiruppur cotton, §2).
5. **Engraved brass initials charm** tied to the cord — cross-sell with §8 BMP Labels; small, high-perceived-value.
6. **Natural void fill = dyed korai shred or dried marigold/petals** instead of wood-wool — on-brand, locally sourced, solves the no-TN-wood-wool gap.
7. **Custom wax seal or branded twill ribbon** ("uru") for the box close — printed-ribbon makers via Sowcarpet (§6).
8. **Fragrance sachet** (incense/potpourri from Sivakasi, §9) tucked in the weave — scent memory of the day.
9. **A reusable rubber/brass hand-stamp** you own, to press names/dates onto kraft tags in-house — turns personalisation into a fast in-studio step rather than a per-order vendor job.
