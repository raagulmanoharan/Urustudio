What goes inside

The keepsake is the gift you keep — this is the menu of what fills it. Curated, grouped, and always chosen to the occasion. Sweets, seeds and soap sit at the heart; everything else is an accent. Every item is gift-scale, sized to the woven keepsake it goes into.

How to fill a set

Pick one hero filling + one or two accents. Keep it plastic-free. We confirm shelf-life and lead time per item.

Sized to the keepsake

Nothing over ~10 cm. Drawstring styles hold ~250–400 g total; flat items (soap, cards) suit the reed clutch. We size each item to the style you choose.

Made to order

Nothing off a shelf. Fresh items are made near your date; keepsakes and dry goods can be prepared ahead.

Sourced in Tamil Nadu

Soap (Coimbatore), sweets & dry fruit (Chennai), brass (Nachiarkoil/Kumbakonam), incense (Sivakasi), seed paper (Avinashi).
