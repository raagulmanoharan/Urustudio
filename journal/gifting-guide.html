A short guide to gifting by occasion

Weddings are only the beginning. Here is how we think about filling a basket for the other days worth marking.

The return gift is not only a wedding thing. Any day that gathers your people is a day worth sending them home with something. Here is how we think about filling a basket for each one.

Weddings and receptions

The full thamboolam: betel and areca, a coconut, turmeric and kumkum, jasmine, and a sweet or two. Woven to your colours, from fifty guests to a few thousand.

Housewarmings

Something for the new home. A small brass lamp, mango leaves for the doorway, a folded cloth, and a sweet to share on the first evening under the roof.

Festivals and poojas

Seasonal and auspicious. A brass diya, incense, a little turmeric and kumkum, and the sweets the festival asks for, ready in time for the calendar.

Baby and naming

Gentle and small. Glass bangles for a valaikaappu, a soft cloth, a keepsake to grow up with. Nothing loud, everything kept.

Corporate and bulk

Gifting at volume that still feels made by hand. Dry fruit and mithai in a clean box, a coaster set, a card. The same care, a few hundred at a time.

Not sure what fits? Tell us the occasion and we will suggest a filling.
