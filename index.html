Keepsakes Worth keeping

Handwoven return gifts, made to order for weddings across South India. Reed, cotton, clay. No plastic in the gift.

See the pieces

Uru means to make, to give something form.

A wedding takes a year to plan and is over in a single day. What we make is the part that stays. A handwoven keepsake, small enough to keep and good enough to use, that sends everyone who came home with a little of your happiest day, and brings you to mind every time they reach for it.

Signed by Hand

Every piece leaves a loom with a name behind it. We work with women-led clusters across Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, and Kerala: small groups, seasonal work, and pay we'll state plainly. Order a batch and you keep a craft in practice for another season.

Made to Order

Nothing sits in a warehouse, and nothing is pulled off a shelf. The day you confirm, your batch begins. Reed soaked, cotton dyed, each piece woven, filled, and finished by hand for your day alone. What reaches your guests was made for the occasion, not grabbed on the way out. The kind of gift that quietly shows it was thought about.

  1. 01Begun on your word. Nothing exists until you set the date.
  2. 02Woven for your day. By hand, in small batches, in women-led clusters.
  3. 03Made personal. A name, a date, or a line of your own, before it ships.
