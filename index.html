The craft

Made by hand

Every piece begins with natural materials — palm leaf, handspun cotton, brass, and dried botanicals — gathered from makers across South India. It is woven, filled, and finished entirely by hand, then wrapped with a tassel and a note. Nothing is hurried; no two are ever quite the same.

Our story

We wanted the last thing a guest carried home to be the thing they never gave away.

Uru began with a small frustration: the return gift that ends up in a drawer, then a bin. So we set out to make the opposite — something useful, beautiful, and rooted in craft. We work with women-led artisan clusters across South India, in small batches, with natural materials and fair, honest making. Every order keeps a traditional skill in practice, and sends a guest home with something worth keeping.
