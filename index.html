How it Works

Nothing sits in a warehouse and nothing comes off a shelf. Every set is made to order — four steps from the style you choose to the day it arrives.

  1. 1

    Pick the style

    Choose your keepsake from the woven styles you've just seen.

  2. 2

    Customise the contents

    Fill it your way — sweets, seeds, soap — with a name or date pressed in by hand.

  3. 3

    Decide the quantity

    From fifty pieces to two thousand; we confirm the price for your count.

  4. 4

    Made & delivered

    Woven, filled and finished for your date, then delivered before the day.

Signed by Hand

Every piece is made by hand, not machine. We work with skilled weaving vendors across Tamil Nadu, where korai has been woven for generations. Order a batch and you keep a living craft in practice for another season.
